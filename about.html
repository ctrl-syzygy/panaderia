we have Panaderia at home?

Filipino food is more than just something we eat. It's something we share.

Welcome to We Have Panaderia at Home, a little corner dedicated to the Filipino breads, pastries, and treats that make home feel like home.Filipino panaderias have always been more than just places to buy bread. They are part of everyday life—from grabbing a warm piece of bread for breakfast to sharing merienda with family and friends. Our website brings that familiar feeling into your own kitchen.

why we made this?

We created this space to celebrate the simple joy of homemade Filipino pastries. Whether you’re an experienced baker or just starting out, our recipes are here to help you recreate beloved favorites and discover new ones along the way. From Spanish Bread and Pan de Coco to Palipit and Bibingka, each recipe carries a little piece of Filipino culture, comfort, and nostalgia.

it's more than just recipes

Behind every pastry is a story. Some remind us of childhood mornings. Some bring back memories of buying merienda after school. Others are enjoyed during celebrations and family gatherings.

We Have Panaderia at Home is our way of keeping those little traditions alive—one homemade pastry at a time.
